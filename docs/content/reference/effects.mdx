title: Effects
description: Every effect in the fx package, its fields, and whether it suits a transition, a backdrop, or feedback.
---

All effects are in [`github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx). Each takes a
progress `t` from 0 to 1:

- As a **transition** (`bloom.Transition`, `bloom.Present`, `Layer.Presence`, `Layer.Transition`), it changes the
  node's own cells. `t` runs from 0, starting to enter, to 1, fully shown and unchanged.
- As a **backdrop** or **shadow** (`Layer.Backdrop`, `Layer.Shadow`), it changes the frame beneath a layer. `t` is
  the strength, following the layer's transition.
- Some take their own progress, such as a shake's or a burst's, and are applied with `bloom.Transition` while it runs.
- As a **border effect** (`Box.BorderFx`), it changes only the box's frame, not what's inside it or on its edges.

`Direction` fields take a side, `bloom.Top`, `bloom.Bottom`, `bloom.Left`, or `bloom.Right`, or a corner,
`bloom.TopLeft`, `bloom.TopRight`, `bloom.BottomLeft`, or `bloom.BottomRight` (also available as `fx.Top` and so on).
From a corner, effects that sweep across a node (`Wipe`, `Decode`, `Blinds`, `Wave`, `Shimmer`) run diagonally, at
45 degrees as the terminal draws it; `Slide` moves in from beyond the corner, and `Stagger` takes it as its top or
bottom side. The zero value is the effect's default: `Bottom`, unless the table says otherwise. `Seed` fields pick a
random pattern that stays the same for the same seed.

The [effects example](https://github.com/lrstanley/bloom/tree/master/_examples/effects) loops every effect as a transition, then shows each backdrop over them:

![The effects example: a gallery of every effect entering and leaving, then the Dim, Desaturate, Tint, and Recolor backdrops in turn](/demos/effects.webp)

## Entrances

| Effect | Fields | Brings a node in by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Fade`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Fade) | | crossfading from what's beneath it |
| [`Slide`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Slide) | `From Direction` | moving in from beyond a side or corner of its viewport |
| [`Wipe`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Wipe) | `From Direction` | revealing it from one side or corner to the opposite one |
| [`Dissolve`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Dissolve) | `Seed` | revealing its cells in a random order |
| [`Typewriter`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Typewriter) | | revealing its cells in reading order |
| [`Scramble`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Scramble) | `Seed` | random glyphs settling into its own |
| [`Decode`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Decode) | `From Direction` (default `Top`), `Seed` | columns falling into place like digital rain, or rows or diagonals from another side or corner |
| [`Unfold`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Unfold) | | opening from its middle row outward, like a CRT turning on |
| [`Iris`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Iris) | `At image.Point` | a circle growing from `At` (screen coordinates; zero is the node's center) |
| [`Shade`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Shade) | `Seed` | each cell stepping through ░ ▒ ▓ to its glyph |
| [`Blinds`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Blinds) | `From Direction`, `Size int` (default 3) | opening slats, like window blinds; diagonal from a corner |
| [`Wave`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Wave) | `From Direction` | a wobbling front sweeping across, with a bright crest |
| [`Glitch`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Glitch) | `Seed` | a breaking-up signal that settles, with jitter and split colors |

## Backdrops

| Effect | Fields | Changes what's beneath a layer by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Dim`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Dim) | `Amount float64` | moving colors toward the terminal's background |
| [`Desaturate`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Desaturate) | `Amount float64` | moving colors toward gray |
| [`Tint`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Tint) | `Color color.Color`, `Amount float64` | moving colors toward `Color` |
| [`Recolor`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Recolor) | `Fg, Bg color.Color` | drawing every cell in two colors, keeping the glyphs |
| [`Spotlight`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Spotlight) | `Rect image.Rectangle`, `Amount float64` (default 0.7), `Soft int` | dimming everything outside `Rect`, with a soft edge `Soft` cells wide |

`Amount` runs from 0, no change, to 1, all the way. Backdrops also work as transitions; `Tint` on a node fades it in
from a color, for example.

## Feedback

These take their own progress rather than an entrance's. See [Feedback effects](/motion/feedback-effects) for how to
drive them.

| Effect | Fields | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Shake`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Shake) | `Amount int` (default 2), `Times int` (default 4) | moves the node side to side with a decaying wobble; still at 0 and 1 |
| [`Shimmer`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Shimmer) | `Color color.Color`, `Width int` (default 6), `From Direction` (default `TopLeft`) | sweeps a highlight band across the node, parallel to a side or diagonal from a corner; `t` is the band's position |
| [`Roll`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Roll) | `From string` | turns digits over like an odometer, from the number in `From` |
| [`Burst`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Burst) | `At image.Point`, `Count int` (default 40), `Seed` | throws confetti from `At`; `t` is its age, gone at 1 |

## Moves

These draw a slot or layer on its way to a new place, given to
[`SlotNode.MoveWith`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom#SlotNode.MoveWith) or
[`LayerNode.MoveWith`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom#LayerNode.MoveWith). See
[Layout animation](/motion/layout-animation#move-with-effects).

| Effect | Fields | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Fly`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Fly) | `Seed` | flies the node's cells over from where it was, a column at a time, as bright glyphs arcing to where they land |

## Borders

These change only the cells around the edge of what they're given, which for `Box.BorderFx` is the border. They
loop: `t` is the phase, such as from `anim.NewLoop`, and they look the same at 0 and 1. See
[Animate a border](/layout/text-and-boxes#animate-a-border).

| Effect | Fields | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Chase`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Chase) | `From, To color.Color`, `Cycles int` (default 1) | a gradient from `From` to `To` and back, travelling clockwise; more `Cycles` make a ripple of narrower bands |
| [`Pulse`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Pulse) | `Color color.Color` | moves the whole border toward `Color` and back, reaching it at 0.5 |
| [`Comet`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Comet) | `Color color.Color`, `Length int` (default 8) | sends a bright head with a fading tail once around |
| [`March`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#March) | `Length int` (default 2) | marching ants: runs of line glyphs alternate with dashed ones and travel clockwise, a run each loop |

A nil `From` is each cell's own color, and other nil colors are the terminal's foreground. Other effects work as
border effects too, on the whole frame, such as `Tint` or `Shimmer`. Effects that move cells don't move the frame.

## Combinators

These wrap other effects.

| Effect | Fields | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`Stagger`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Stagger) | `Effect`, `From Direction`, `Spread float64` (default 0.5) | runs `Effect` on each row (`From` top or bottom) or column (left or right), one after another; a corner is taken as its top or bottom |
| [`Delay`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Delay) | `Effect`, `From, To float64` (`To` defaults to 1) | runs `Effect` only between `From` and `To` of the progress |
| [`Reverse`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Reverse) | `Effect` | runs `Effect` with `1-t` |
| [`Ease`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Ease) | `Effect`, `Easing func(float64) float64` | runs `Effect` with eased progress, such as `anim.OutBack` |

## Your own

| Type | Does |
| --- | --- |
| [`Func`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/lrstanley/bloom/fx#Func) | turns a `func(*canvas.Surface, float64)` into an effect |

Any type with an `Apply(*canvas.Surface, float64)` method is an effect. See [Custom effects](/motion/custom-effects).

## Things to know

- Effects listed together run in order with the same `t`.
- Effects that need the terminal's colors (`Dim`, `Shimmer` with no color, `Wave`) work best when the root has
  heard them: return `m.ui.Init()` from `Init` and pass messages to `m.ui.Update`.
- Effects that replace glyphs (`Scramble`, `Decode`, `Shade`, `Glitch`) leave wide characters, such as emoji, alone or
  reveal them in one step, so they never draw half a character.
- `Ease` and `Func` hold functions, which can't be compared, so frames using them are redrawn every time. Keep them
  on nodes that are animating.
