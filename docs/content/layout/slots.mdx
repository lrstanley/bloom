---
title: Sizing with slots
description: Control how children of a row or column share space with Fixed, Auto, Fill, Grow, Percent, Min, Max, and Space.
---

A `bloom.Row` places children left to right, and a `bloom.Column` places them top to bottom. How much of the
row or column each child gets is decided by the **slot** you wrap it in. The child doesn't choose; whoever builds
the layout does. That's why the same component can be a fixed-width sidebar in one screen and fill the space in
another.

```go
bloom.Row(
	bloom.Fixed(10, nav),   // exactly 10 columns
	toolbar,                // no slot: its natural width
	bloom.Fill(editor),     // a share of what's left
	bloom.Grow(2, preview), // two shares of what's left
)
```

│fixed   ││auto││fill        ││grow 2                      │

## Choose a slot

| Slot | Size | Grows | Shrinks when space is short |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`Fixed(n, c)`](https://pkg.go.dev/bloomfx.dev/bloom#Fixed) | exactly `n` cells | no | no |
| [`Auto(c)`](https://pkg.go.dev/bloomfx.dev/bloom#Auto), or `c` with no slot | the child's natural size | no | yes, down to its `Min` |
| [`Fill(c)`](https://pkg.go.dev/bloomfx.dev/bloom#Fill) | an equal share of the space left over | yes | yes, down to its `Min` |
| [`Grow(w, c)`](https://pkg.go.dev/bloomfx.dev/bloom#Grow) | `w` shares of the space left over | yes, by weight | yes, down to its `Min` |
| [`Percent(p, c)`](https://pkg.go.dev/bloomfx.dev/bloom#Percent) | `p`% of the row or column | no | yes, down to its `Min` |

"Space left over" is what remains after the `Fixed`, `Auto`, and `Percent` slots, and the gaps, are placed. `Fill`
is the same as `Grow(1, c)`.

A child's natural size is the space its content needs: the width of its longest line and its number of lines for
text, plus the border and padding for a box. See [How layout works](/concepts/how-layout-works) for how bloom
measures a child, and how a component can report its own size.

## Set a minimum and a maximum

`Min` and `Max` clamp a slot on the row or column's direction:

```go
bloom.Row(
	bloom.Fill(sidebar).Max(32), // grows, but never past 32 columns
	bloom.Fill(editor).Min(40),  // shrinks, but never below 40 columns
)
```

The minimum is zero unless you set one, so without `Min` a slot can be squeezed to nothing. When the minimums
don't all fit, see [Responsive layouts](/layout/responsive) for dropping slots with `Collapsible`.

## Leave empty space

- `bloom.Fixed(n, nil)` is `n` empty cells, such as a blank line in a column.
- `bloom.Space()` is empty space that grows, which pushes the children after it to the far end.

```go
bloom.Row(bloom.Text("left"), bloom.Space(), bloom.Text("right"))
```

```
left                     right
```

To put the same space between every pair of children, use `Gap`, described in
[Text, boxes, and alignment](/layout/text-and-boxes#space-children-evenly).

## Slots in a column

Everything above applies to columns too, with heights instead of widths. A common screen shape is a header and
a status bar at their natural height, and the content filling the middle:

```go
bloom.Column(
	header,
	bloom.Fill(content),
	statusbar,
)
```

<Callout type="idea" title="A column is as tall as the screen">
  The outermost node gets the whole window. A column that should stretch its middle child needs that child in a
  `Fill` slot; without one, the children sit at the top at their natural heights.
</Callout>

## Give a slot an ID

Slots are where the other per-child options live: `ID` for focus and lookups, `On` for
[mouse handlers](/input/mouse), `Focusable` and `Scope` for [focus](/input/focus), `Align` for the cross axis, and
`Animate` for [layout animation](/motion/layout-animation).

```go
bloom.Fixed(24, sidebar).ID(paneSidebar).Collapsible(1)
```
